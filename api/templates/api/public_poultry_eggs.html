Poultry investor participation

Follow the flock and understand the numbers.

A privately agreed poultry participation arrangement can assign capital to a specific flock or batch. RayNoor's management records help trace contributions, purchases, losses, egg or bird sales and realized financial results.

Define the batch

Agree on the flock, investment contribution, ownership, operating expenses and sharing rules.

Record spending

Chicks, feed, medication, labour and mortality are recorded with the flock.

Review results

Authorized participants can receive agreed production, cost and sale performance statements.

Calculate settlement

Once sales and costs are reconciled, determine actual profit or loss according to the agreement.

No guaranteed returns. Disease, mortality, feed prices and sale rates may result in losses. Investor arrangements require written terms, appropriate eligibility and legal review; the public site does not accept investments.
