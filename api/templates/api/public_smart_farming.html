Environmental monitoring

Temperature and humidity from supported sensor-equipped sheds, viewed alongside farm records so problems are easier to notice.

Electrical and ventilation controls

Supported lighting, fans and environmental equipment can be managed using configured local controls and authorized remote interfaces. Welfare checks remain essential.

Goats and poultry together

Animal weight and breeding, flock production, feed and medicine, inventory, cash movements and investor statements are organized in the farm platform.

AI-assisted decision support

AI may help summarize trends or flag records worth attention. AI is not a substitute for farm supervision, veterinary advice or authenticated measurements.
