Understanding participation

Participate with clarity, not promises.

A transparent process

How a private project could be structured.

Written agreement

Identify who owns the animals, what each party contributes, the decision rights, risk-sharing and the settlement formula.

Contribution records

Log capital, birds or goats purchased, receipts, financing and any further approved contributions.

Ongoing reporting

Capture feed, medicine, mortality, weights, egg production and realized sales when applicable.

Actual settlement

Reconcile sale receipts, costs, liabilities and withdrawals; distribute or settle according to the signed agreement.

Two different livestock models

Participation for goats and poultry.

Goat participation

A defined goat or livestock project may track purchase value, feed, veterinary costs, weight progress, breeding decisions and final sale value.

Profit or loss is not simply sale price minus purchase price; actual agreed costs and any liabilities matter.

Explore goat programme

Poultry participation

A defined flock or layer project may track chicks, feed, medication, mortality, egg/bird sales, costs and remaining stock.

Investor distributions should depend on the flock's reconciled position and contract, not on cash receipts alone.

Important questions to settle first

Transparency includes the difficult questions.

Animal & market risks

Livestock may become ill or die. Feed and medicine prices can rise, and sale prices may fall. Loss of capital is possible.

Ownership & care

Document animal ownership, insurance (if any), veterinary responsibility, authority for emergency care, exits and Qurbani or sale arrangements.

Legal & financial terms

Have an appropriate professional review contracts, eligibility, any required approvals, taxes and what financial information may be shared.

No guaranteed or fixed returns. This site does not display live investment opportunities and cannot accept investments. Existing farm dashboards remain private. Any future participation is subject to individual review, written terms and applicable rules.

Remote reporting is part of our approach.

Authorized participants may receive their agreed reports while away from the farm. A public self-enrolment and dedicated customer-investor portal are not launched.
