Staff Salaries & Payroll

Fixed monthly salaries are for staff employees only. Salary is an operating expense, independent of salesperson fuel and commission deductions. Existing commission earnings and payable balances remain in the old ledger. Only administrators may post pay or advances.

Switching from commission to salary: Close the earlier commission dates first. If a paid commission period overlaps the salary start, an administrator must document the salary-credit portion. Posted commissions and historical payments stay intact; never pay the same amount twice.

Administrators: Record payments to admin and iqramanzoor individually using Variable Admin Payments below. Select remuneration for actual wages or owner drawing for an owner's personal withdrawal. Superuser access alone does not establish ownership.
