Automatic Egg POS receipts: payments physically handed over as Cash are added to farm cash in hand automatically; bank/wallet payments go to Bank/Wallet instead. The same Egg POS payment is never entered again here. New feed, medicine, and other farm costs marked Paid From Farm Management Cash also deduct cash automatically. A Rs 150 cash payment is an increase in cash, not additional farm sales. These balances are only complete when opening balances, expenses and withdrawals have been recorded. Direct cash sales are treated as received by the farm; if staff still holds that cash, it requires separate reconciliation.
