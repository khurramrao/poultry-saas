Outbound delivery: sale to customer

When RayNoor pays a rider or courier for a completed retail sale, choose Customer Delivery / Courier Charges in the expense category below. Include the POS invoice number in Receipt / Reference. This debits Delivery / Rider Expense and credits the selected cash/bank account.

Don't enter it here again if you already posted the same delivery expense under Fuel / Staff Expenses. Fees charged to customers are sales/delivery income, not this expense.
Inbound transportation: goods received

Enter delivery/transport paid to bring stock into RayNoor in the Inbound Transport (Rs) field of the related purchase or farm transfer, not this expense form. It increases FIFO landed stock cost and posts the matching cash/bank/supplier payable entry once.
